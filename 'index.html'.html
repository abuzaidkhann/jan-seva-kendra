Common Sawaal

Aapke Sawaalon Ke Jawaab

GST registration mein kitna time lagta hai?
+
Normal GST registration 3 se 7 working days mein ho jaata hai, documents poore hone par. Humari team application submit karne ke baad aapko WhatsApp par update deti rehti hai.
Mujhe kaun se documents lekar aane hain?
+
Service ke hisaab se documents alag hote hain — jaise GST ke liye PAN, Aadhaar, business proof, aur bank details chahiye. Call ya WhatsApp karne par hum aapko exact list bata denge, taaki ek hi baar mein sab sahi documents le aayein.
Kya ghar baithe online kaam ho sakta hai?
+
Haan bilkul. Zyaadatar services (GST, ITR, PAN, PF) WhatsApp par documents bhej kar online ho jaati hain. Sirf kuch services mein biometric ya original document dikhana padta hai, uske liye kendra par aana hoga.
Fees kitni lagti hai aur kab deni hoti hai?
+
Har service ki fees pehle hi clearly bata di jaati hai — koi hidden charge nahi hota. Zyaadatar cases mein payment kaam shuru hone se pehle ya poora hone ke baad liya jaata hai, jaisa aap chahein.
Agar mera kaam reject ho jaaye to kya hoga?
+
Chinta mat kijiye — agar kisi vajah se application reject hoti hai, to hum reason check karke dobara sahi tarike se file karte hain. Isme koi extra professional fee nahi li jaati.
Status kaise pata karu ki mera kaam kahan tak pahuncha?
+
Upar "Track Status" button par click karke apna registered mobile number daalein, turant status dikh jaayega. Ya seedha WhatsApp par bhi pooch sakte hain.
